VIP Service Agreement Scroll to read all terms

1. Scope of VIP Services: Sour Apple Wash & Fold VIP Laundry Services provides premier wash, dry, and fold care. Customers agree to submit laundry strictly conforming to these terms.

2. Strict Bag Closure Policy: All laundry MUST be contained in closed bags fastened with drawstrings, zippers, Velcro, or snaps. Open baskets, unlidded hampers, and loose piles are strictly rejected for sanitation and transport safety.

3. STRICT ZERO-TOLERANCE INFESTATION & CONTAMINATION POLICY: We maintain an uncompromising zero-tolerance policy regarding pests and biohazards. Laundry containing bedbugs, fleas, lice, roaches, mold, pet waste, bodily fluids, or hazardous chemicals is strictly prohibited. Any load discovered with pests or biohazards will be immediately halted, double-bagged in airtight plastic, and returned without refund. A mandatory $50.00 hazardous decontamination handling fee will be assessed.

4. Turnaround & Curbside Delivery: Standard processing is 48 to 72 hours. Same-day rush (drop-off before 10:00 AM, ready after 5:00 PM) is subject to availability. Campus pickups for MVCC students, staff, and faculty occur at designated campus curbside checkpoints.

5. Wash Care & Pocket Checks: We follow your selected water temperature (Cold, Warm, Hot) and detergent choice. Customers MUST inspect all pockets and remove pens, markers, coins, cosmetics, and sharp objects prior to submission. We are not liable for damage caused by items left in pockets or natural fabric dye-bleeding.

6. Limitation of Liability: In the rare event of verified loss or processing damage, Sour Apple’s liability is strictly limited to a maximum of $100.00 per bag. High-value, delicate dry-clean-only garments, or irreplaceable sentimental items should not be submitted.

7. Unclaimed Laundry & 30-Day Charity Donation: Orders not retrieved within 30 calendar days from the scheduled completion date will be deemed abandoned and donated to local Utica community charities.

8. Electronic Signature: Checking the agreement box and typing your legal name below constitutes a legally binding electronic signature under the federal ESIGN Act and NY Electronic Signatures and Records Act.
