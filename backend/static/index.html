VIP Service Agreement Scroll to read all terms

1. Scope of VIP Services: Sour Apple Wash & Fold VIP Laundry Services provides premier wash, dry, and fold care. Customers agree to place laundry in bags meeting our strict closure rules.

2. Strict Bag Closure Policy: All laundry MUST be contained in closed bags fastened with drawstrings, zippers, Velcro, or snaps. Open baskets, unlidded hampers, and loose piles are strictly rejected for sanitation and transport safety.

3. Turnaround & Curbside Delivery: Standard processing is 48 to 72 hours. Same-day rush (drop-off before 10:00 AM, ready after 5:00 PM) is subject to availability. Campus pickups for MVCC students, staff, and faculty will occur at verified curbside dorm checkpoints.

4. Wash Care & Pocket Check: We follow your selected water temperature (Cold, Warm, Hot) and detergent choice (Standard VIP, Fragrance-Free, or Customer-Provided). Customers MUST inspect all pockets and remove pens, markers, coins, cosmetics, and sharp objects prior to drop-off. We are not responsible for damage caused by items left in pockets or natural fabric wear/dye-bleeding.

5. Limitation of Liability: In the rare event of verified loss or processing damage, Sour Apple’s liability is strictly limited to a maximum of $100.00 per bag. High-value, delicate dry-clean-only garments, or irreplaceable sentimental items should not be submitted.

6. Unclaimed Laundry & 30-Day Charity Donation: Orders not retrieved within 30 calendar days from the scheduled completion date will be deemed abandoned and donated to local Utica community charities.

7. Electronic Signature: Checking the agreement box and typing your legal name below constitutes a legally binding electronic signature under the federal ESIGN Act and NY Electronic Signatures and Records Act.
